About Our Menu

This is a digital restaurant menu that lets you browse and search food items across three categories: Main Course, Desserts, and Drinks. You can see further details upon clicking the item, which opens a card.

What's on the Menu

We have 12 items throughout Main Course, Desserts, and Drinks. Each item includes a name, price, image, and a nutritional breakdown.

How to Search

Use the search bar on the menu page to filter items by name. You can also use the category buttons to filter by Main, Desserts, or Drinks.

Nutrition Info

Click any menu card to expand it and see the full nutrition details including calories, protein, carbs, and fat.
